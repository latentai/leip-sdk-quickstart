# How-to Guides

If you have a specific task in mind and are already familiar with LEIP Design, these guides contain more in-depth material than our tutorials. They will help you achieve an end result but may require you to adapt steps to fit your specific requirements.

[**Bring Your Own Data (BYOD)**](BYOD.ipynb): How to integrate your own dataset into a LEIP Design recipe.

[**GRDB Model Training and Optimization**](GRDB_Model_Training_and_Optimization.ipynb): How to train multiple candidate recipes, evaluate and select the best performer, and visualize predictions.

[**From Classifier GRDB to Deployable Artifacts**](Classifier_GRDB_E2E.ipynb): A step-by-step guide to loading a recipe from the Classifier GRDB, training the model, and generating deployable artifacts using LEIP Design.

[**Optimize**](Optimize.ipynb): How to use the LEIP Design API to explore LEIP Optimize's compilation, quantization, and calibration parameters.

[**Explore Golden Volumes**](ExploreGoldenVolumes.ipynb): How to select a recipe from the Golden Recipe Database (GRDB).

[**Setup Offline Mode**](setup_offline_mode.ipynb): How to configure LEIP Design for offline use, ensuring all necessary components are cached locally for use without a network connection.

[**Ingest dataset from FiftyOne**](Attach_FiftyOne_tutorial.ipynb): How to ingest a dataset hosted in FiftyOne into your LEIP recipe.
